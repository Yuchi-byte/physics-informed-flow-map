Flow-Map Priors with Inference-Time Tilting for Full-Waveform Inversion

Research plan & idea · 2026-06-25 · physics-informed-flow-map

Learn a generative prior over subsurface velocity fields with flow matching; distill it into a Meta Flow Map for fast, steerable few-step sampling; then solve FWI as inference-time posterior sampling — tilting the prior toward observed seismic data through the wave-equation likelihood. Physics enters at inference, not training.

Thesis. The wave-equation data fit $\|F(v)-d\|^2$ is a measurement-dependent likelihood, so it belongs at inference, not in the prior's training loss. A flow-map prior makes that inference cheap: posterior sampling needs only a handful of forward solves (vs. hundreds for diffusion DPS / RED-DiffEq) and yields calibrated uncertainty that supervised inverters (InversionNet) cannot. The bet: match or beat diffusion-based posterior FWI on accuracy, at a fraction of the forward solves, with usable uncertainty.

This sits in camp A (prior + inference-time guidance) of the prior-work taxonomy — the same family as RED-DiffEq and DPS-FWI — but with a flow-map prior and Meta-Flow-Map steering rather than a diffusion model. The unconditional velocity prior already exists and is tuned (clean FlatVel-A samples, energy distance ≈1.4× the real-vs-real floor).

Distill → Meta Flow Map

→
core
Stage 3

Tilt toward measurements

Sample the posterior $p(v\mid d)$ by steering the flow map with the wave-equation likelihood (reward $=-\|F(v)-d\|^2$). Output: multiple data-consistent velocity maps with uncertainty.

Prerequisite spanning Stage 3: a differentiable forward operator $F$ (Deepwave) and seismic data $d$ (download or simulate from held-out maps). This is the one missing build.

Formulation

Prior interpolant (mfm convention): $x_t=(1-t)\,\epsilon+t\,v$, with $\epsilon\sim\mathcal N(0,I)$, $t{=}1$ the clean velocity map; learned velocity field $u_\theta(x_t,t)$, ODE $\dot x=u_\theta(x,t)$.

Target posterior:

$$p(v\mid d)\;\propto\;\underbrace{p(v)}_{\text{flow-map prior}}\;\cdot\;\underbrace{\exp\!\Big(-\tfrac{1}{2\sigma^2}\,\|F(v)-d\|^2\Big)}_{\text{wave-equation likelihood (the tilt)}}.$$

Tilted (guided) velocity used during integration:

$$\tilde u(x_t,t)=u_\theta(x_t,t)+g(t)\,\nabla_{x_t}\log p\big(d\mid\hat v(x_t,t)\big),\qquad \nabla_{x_t}\log p(d\mid\hat v)=-\tfrac1{\sigma^2}\Big(\tfrac{\partial \hat v}{\partial x_t}\Big)^{\!\top}\!J_F^{\top}\big(F(\hat v)-d\big),$$

where $\hat v(x_t,t)$ is the flow map's one-shot clean estimate and $J_F=\nabla F$ is the forward-operator Jacobian (Deepwave supplies the VJP). This is DPS ported to flow matching (FlowDPS, FMPS). Equivalently, in Meta-Flow-Map terms the tilt is a reward $r(v)=-\tfrac1{2\sigma^2}\|F(v)-d\|^2$ and Stage 3 is reward-steered sampling — exactly the mechanism of the Meta Flow Maps paper (the mfm package ships scripts/sample_steered.py).

Research axis A · the tilting signal

How to inject the measurement most accurately, stably, and cheaply. This is the heart of the project.

A1 · Guidance formulation

  • Gradient guidance (baseline): flow-Tweedie $\hat v$ + likelihood gradient (FlowDPS, FMPS).
  • Covariance-aware guidance: ΠGDM-style pseudo-inverse / posterior-covariance correction (Song et al. 2023) — usually beats isotropic DPS.
  • Hard data consistency / projection: enforce constraints exactly at steps (PCFM) rather than soft gradients.
  • Gradient-free / particle methods: twisted SMC / sequential Monte Carlo over flow trajectories for better-calibrated posteriors when the gradient is unreliable.

A2 · Misfit design (borrow from classical FWI)

  • Optimal-transport / Wasserstein misfit instead of $L^2$ to convexify and fight cycle skipping (Engquist & Froese, Métivier et al.).
  • Multi-scale / frequency annealing of $d$ during sampling — low frequencies first (Bunks et al.), naturally aligned with going early→late in the flow.
  • Envelope / phase misfits as robust early-stage signals.

A3 · Schedule & cost

  • Guidance-strength schedule $g(t)$: when in the trajectory the tilt is applied, ramping, step-size adaptation.
  • Forward-solve budget: trade #steps × #sources vs. accuracy; exploit the flow map's few-step sampling to minimize PDE solves (the core efficiency claim — quantify solves-to-accuracy vs. diffusion DPS).
  • MFM value amortization: use the flow map's differentiable reparametrization to estimate the reward/value without full rollouts (Potaptchik et al.).

Research axis B · training strategy

B1 · Prior training

  • Data curriculum: FlatVel → CurveVel → Fault → Style families; per-family vs. one joint multi-family prior (broader prior generalizes better OOD, but harder to fit).
  • Interpolant choice: linear/OT path (straight, few-step) vs. VP — straightness matters for both sampling cost and guidance stability.
  • Augmentation (flips, crops, value jitter) for OOD robustness to Marmousi/Overthrust.
  • Already in place: EMA, held-out validation loss, normalization to $[-1,1]$.

B2 · Distillation to the Meta Flow Map

  • GLASS distillation from the FM teacher (mfm's recommended mode) vs. flow-map self-distillation (Boffi et al.).
  • Step-count target: how few steps before posterior quality degrades — the accuracy/cost frontier.
  • Stochastic flow map (i.i.d. clean draws from any state) to support posterior diversity, not just a point map.

B3 · What NOT to put in training

  • The wave-equation data fit (measurement-dependent → inference only).
  • Optional, secondary: a light measurement-independent regularizer (value range, smoothness priors) — mostly already handled by normalization; PBFM/PIDM-style training physics is largely unnecessary for FWI.
  • Complementary baseline: also train an amortized conditional $p(v\mid d)$ (camp B) to compare tilting against one-pass conditional generation.

Research axis C · architecture

  • Backbone: current DiT (256/6/8, patch 4 @ 64×64); sweep patch size / token count (we found 200 sampler steps optimal, 500 worse — interacts with backbone capacity). Compare DiT vs. SongUNet for fields.
  • Attention backend: SDPA → FlashAttention-2/3; RoPE positional embedding for spatial generalization.
  • Resolution: 64 now; native 70; 3D seismic cubes later (the real target geometry).
  • Latent vs pixel: a VAE latent space for higher resolution / 3D, vs. pixel-space for fidelity at current scale.
  • Conditioning hooks: mechanism for the amortized conditional baseline (B3) and for any learned guidance — adaLN-Zero / cross-attention on seismic features or common-image gathers.

Baselines

  • Supervised: InversionNet, VelocityGAN (OpenFWI official).
  • Conditional generative: DiffusionVel; I2SB / conditional rectified flow.
  • Posterior / tilting (head-to-head): RED-DiffEq, DPS-FWI — same camp, diffusion prior. Our claim is parity-or-better accuracy at far fewer forward solves, with calibrated uncertainty.

Milestones

  1. M0 ✓Unconditional prior. FM prior on FlatVel-A, tuned, with EMA + held-out val. Done.
  2. M1Forward operator. Integrate Deepwave; simulate $d$ from held-out velocity maps; verify $F$ and its gradient (VJP) end-to-end.
  3. M2Meta Flow Map. Distill the FM prior into a steerable few-step flow map; confirm sample quality at low NFE.
  4. M3Tilting v1. DPS-for-flow / MFM-steered posterior sampling on FlatVel-A; recover held-out maps; report MAE/SSIM + uncertainty + #solves vs. a diffusion-DPS baseline.
  5. M4Improve the tilt. Axis-A sweep — OT misfit, multi-scale annealing, covariance guidance, schedules; cycle-skipping robustness.
  6. M5Scale & generalize. More families, OOD Marmousi/Overthrust, then 3D.

Risks & open questions

  • Cycle skipping survives the prior. $\|F(v)-d\|^2$ is non-convex in $v$ even with a strong prior; the tilt may stall in wrong basins → need misfit design (A2) + schedules (A3).
  • DPS approximation × flow-map. Guidance uses the one-shot $\hat v$ as a likelihood proxy; with a few-step flow-map prior this approximation may be looser than with a full diffusion model — quantify the quality/cost trade.
  • Forward-solve cost. Differentiable wave solves dominate; the whole pitch rests on the flow map needing few of them — must be measured, not assumed.
  • Data availability. Real seismic $d$ (~600 GB) was skipped; simulating $d$ with Deepwave from velocity maps is the pragmatic start (and gives clean, controllable experiments).
  • Posterior calibration. Are the tilted draws a faithful posterior, or just diverse point estimates? Needs explicit calibration metrics (Eval).
Where the novelty lives. Camp-A posterior FWI is established for diffusion priors (RED-DiffEq, DPS-FWI). Doing it with a Meta-Flow-Map prior — exploiting few-step sampling and amortized reward steering for cheap, calibrated posterior FWI — is the open contribution, complemented by the axis-A research on the tilting signal itself.
